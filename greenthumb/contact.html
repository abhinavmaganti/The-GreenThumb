How it works

From backyard to community table

Four practical steps to turn a home garden into a source of food, confidence, and local connection.

1

Pick a crop

Start with common favorites like tomatoes, lettuce, peppers, strawberries, basil, beans, and cucumbers.

2

Follow a guide

Use straightforward care guides and a seasonal calendar so you know what to sow, plant, and harvest.

3

Share the surplus

List extra produce, seedlings, seeds, and tools in the marketplace instead of letting them go to waste.

4

Grow with others

Use the map and community feed to learn from nearby gardeners and build a stronger local food network.
