Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the current Green Thumb prototype stores and how that information is handled.

Green Thumb is currently a front-end prototype. Account information, forum posts, and profile edits created in this version are stored locally in your browser using local storage.

We do not currently transmit that prototype account data to a remote database from this version of the site. If you clear your browser storage, your saved prototype data may be lost.

If Green Thumb later adds a hosted backend, authentication provider, analytics, or messaging service, this policy should be updated to reflect what data is collected, how long it is retained, and how users can request deletion or correction.

If you have questions about privacy in the current prototype, use the Contact page in the footer to reach out.
