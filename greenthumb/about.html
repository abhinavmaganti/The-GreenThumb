Meet the creators

Green Thumb was created to make gardening feel more practical, local, and community-driven for everyday growers.

Abhinav

Abhinav focuses on the overall product vision and the practical user experience of Green Thumb. His goal is to make the platform useful for real people trying to grow common foods, share extra produce, and connect with gardeners nearby.

Vishrut

Vishrut helps shape the structure and growth of the project, with attention on how the platform can support gardeners, organize information clearly, and scale into a stronger community experience over time.
